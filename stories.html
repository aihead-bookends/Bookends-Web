Success stories

From one table to seven.

Since 2019, Bookends Hospitality has grown into three brands and seven outlets across Surat and Ahmedabad. These are the moments, the people and the plates behind it.

Read our journey

Our journey

2018 to today.

  1. 2018

    Bookends Hospitality

    Pizzas for friends.

    It started with one person’s love of food. Our founder and CEO, Siddhant Shah, was making pizzas at home for friends and family, and word got around.

  2. 2019

    Dome Pizzeria

    The first café.

    Siddhant opens a small café of his own, Dome Pizzeria: the first Bookends outlet.

  3. 2020

    Dome Pizzeria

    Through the lockdowns.

    When Covid closed the dining rooms, Dome kept its kitchen going on takeaway orders.

  4. 2021

    Soy · Mazal · Olé

    New kitchens.

    Soy opens for Pan-Asian food, followed by Mazal for Lebanese and Olé for Mexican.

  5. 2022

    Capiche · Italian

    Dome becomes Capiche.

    Dome Pizzeria becomes Capiche, and a second Capiche opens at Vesu, Surat.

  6. 2024

    Capiche · Italian

    Into Ahmedabad.

    Capiche opens in Ambli, our first outlet in Ahmedabad, and the Capiche name is trademarked.

  7. 2025

    Aiko · Pan-Asian

    Soy becomes Aiko.

    Soy is reborn as Aiko, with its own trademark, and Aiko Ambli opens in Ahmedabad.

  8. 2026

    Capiche · Italian

    Capiche University Road.

    A fourth Capiche opens, on University Road in Ahmedabad, with breakfast too.

  9. 2026

    Beshak · Indian

    Beshak opens.

    Our first Indian kitchen, on Dumas Road in Surat.

  10. Today

    Bookends Hospitality

    3 brands · 7 outlets · 2 cities

    Aiko, Beshak and Capiche, across Surat and Ahmedabad.

  11. Every good story needs its bookends.
