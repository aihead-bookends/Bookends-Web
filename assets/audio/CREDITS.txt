Intro audio (plays only during the intro on index.html). The live site (main) plays the
Navratri intro: the music and its five voice lines. The other files serve the intro
concepts that are kept in js/openings.js but not shown.

sitar-tabla.m4a     Music: "Sitar Tabla Fusion Beat" by BudgetPixel AI
                    https://budgetpixel.com/background-music/sitar-tabla-fusion-beat-592eb3d7
                    Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
                    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
                    Credit is REQUIRED: it is shown in the footer of index.html.
                    Re-encoded to AAC 96 kbps, otherwise unchanged.

purr.m4a            "Cat purr" (s0436), BigSoundBank, CC0 (public domain)
                    https://bigsoundbank.com/cat-purr-s0436.html
                    A 1.75 s cut (5.25–7.0 s), faded, AAC 64 kbps.

voices/*.m4a        The characters' lines, generated with Kokoro-82M (open-source
                    text-to-speech, Apache License 2.0, https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M):
                    Aiko in English (voice af_bella), Capiche in Italian (im_nicola:
                    "Mio!", "No, io!", "Pronti… via!",
                    "Batti cinque!"…), the tiger in Hindi (hm_omega: "शुभ नवरात्रि!",
                    "शाबाश!", and English lines written in Devanagari for an Indian
                    accent, e.g. "वेल्कम!", "हैप्पी होली!"). Silence trimmed, levelled,
                    AAC 64 kbps. No credit required.

roar.m4a            A 2.6 s cut of "Lion raring-sound1TamilNadu178.ogg", public domain.
                    https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lion_raring-sound1TamilNadu178.ogg

thunder.m4a         A 1.8 s cut of "Tonitrus.ogg", public domain.
                    https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tonitrus.ogg

All other sounds are generated in the browser by js/sound.js: the "!@#$%"
gibberish, punches, slaps, the scuffle in the cloud, poofs, hops, steps,
whooshes, tosses and catches, steam, gulps, munches, the giant cup's thunk and
knocks, the noodle snap, plops and bubbles, sniffs, snores (the purr, slowed),
the door's creak and slam, the high-five clap, the burp, page turns, the
camera shutter and print whirr, boings, the plate crash, the dance notes,
score-card dings, Holi puffs, ta-da, sparkles and the title chime.
